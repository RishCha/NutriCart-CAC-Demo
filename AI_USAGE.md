# AI Usage Disclosure

ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex were used as development assistants for NutriCart. Their assistance included brainstorming, implementation suggestions, debugging, test creation, UX review, privacy and data-boundary review, and documentation editing.

## Student Direction and Review

The student chose the problem, requirements, supported workflows, design priorities, and submission goals. The student reviewed generated changes, tested the application, identified integration and usability problems, and accepted, revised, or rejected suggestions. AI output was not treated as automatically correct.

The student made the final decisions about grocery-planning constraints, diet behavior, location privacy, pricing claims, meal-plan limits, shopping-budget behavior, accessibility, and public-release scope.

## Data Safeguards

The public demonstration catalog was assembled independently from USDA FoodData Central records. Nutrition values come from USDA source data; package examples and prices are explicitly synthetic. AI tools were not used to invent retailer availability, observed prices, nutrition values, or product eligibility.

Account persistence is disabled in the hosted public demo. Private catalogs, credentials, environment files, and local databases are not part of this release.
