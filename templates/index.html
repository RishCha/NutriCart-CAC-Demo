NutriCart uses a general nutrition baseline when these fields are left blank. Enter your own goals or use household-based suggested targets.

How are targets estimated?

Current general guidance is the Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025–2030. Calorie suggestions use broad federal age, reference-category, and activity planning bands derived from EER methods; exact current EER calculations require height and weight, which NutriCart does not collect. Protein and fiber use age- and life-stage Dietary Reference Intakes.

These are general planning references for generally healthy people, not exact requirements or medical advice.
